4. Design Rules for PCB

  • Keep Analog and Digital grounds separated; star-point under the package centre.
  • SWAT differential pair must be length-matched ±50 µm and routed away from high-speed I/O.
  • Place 100 nF X7R + 10 µF bulk at every VCC_ANALOG / VCC_DIGITAL ball within 3 mm.
  • Clock phases are source-synchronous; treat as low-skew group.
  • TX-9942 relay coil side must be galvanically isolated; only the sense/force pins cross the isolation barrier.
